# Retail Business Performance & Profitability Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Retail Business Performance & Profitability Dashboard** is an interactive data analytics project built using Microsoft Excel. It transforms retail sales data into a structured dashboard to help analyze revenue, profitability, product performance, regional sales, and customer segments.

The project demonstrates an end-to-end Excel analytics workflow, from data preparation to business reporting and visualization.

## Business Objectives

- Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
- Calculate key performance indicators (KPIs).
- Compare sales across product categories and regions.
- Identify profitable and loss-making product sub-categories.
- Understand monthly sales trends.
- Compare revenue contributions across customer segments.
- Present business information through an interactive dashboard.

## Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Excel** — analysis, calculations, and dashboard development
- **Power Query** — data cleaning and transformation
- **PivotTables** — aggregation and business analysis
- **PivotCharts** — visual representation of analytical results
- **Slicers** — interactive filtering
- **Excel formulas** — KPI calculations

## Project Workflow

### 1. Data Preparation
- Imported the retail sales dataset into Excel.
- Used Power Query to clean and transform the data.
- Reviewed date formats and data consistency.
- Loaded the prepared data for analysis.

### 2. Exploratory Analysis
Created PivotTables to analyze:
- Sales by category
- Sales by region
- Profit by sub-category
- Monthly sales trends
- Sales by customer segment

### 3. KPI Development

The dashboard focuses on the following metrics:

| KPI | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Total Sales | Sum of Sales |
| Total Profit | Sum of Profit |
| Profit Margin | Total Profit / Total Sales |
| Total Orders | Distinct count of Order ID |

### 4. Dashboard Development

Designed an executive-style dashboard featuring:
- KPI summary cards
- Monthly sales trend chart
- Category sales visualization
- Regional sales comparison
- Sub-category profitability chart
- Customer segment analysis
- Interactive slicers for filtering data

## Business Value

This dashboard provides a consolidated view of retail performance and helps users explore:

- Which product categories contribute most to revenue
- How sales vary across regions
- Which sub-categories generate profit or losses
- How sales performance changes over time
- How different customer segments contribute to revenue

These analyses can support further investigation into pricing, product performance, and regional business opportunities.
